INC-003 — Retrospective note: fault-time SMB share permissions
Date documented: 2026-10-13
Share: DeptShare
Server: LAB-DC01
Share path: C:\LabShares\DeptShare

Evidence type: Retrospective summary, NOT a contemporaneous transcript.

Source and limitation:
During the incident session, the output of Get-SmbShareAccess for
DeptShare was observed after Revoke-SmbShareAccess removed the
AD\Lab.TestUser share permission. The observed output showed
AD\Domain Admins with Allow / Full access, and no AD\Lab.TestUser
entry. The fault-time output was not saved directly to a file at
that moment. This note records that observed result from the session;
it is not a raw capture and should not be represented as one.

Related contemporaneous evidence:
- INC-003_fault-negative-access.txt
- INC-003_fault-ntfs-acl-check.txt

Interpretation:
The client received Access is denied when attempting to create a file
during the fault. The NTFS ACL inspection during the fault matched
the baseline ACL. Together with the observed share ACL, these results
support the conclusion that the simulated fault was the removal of
the user's SMB share permission.
